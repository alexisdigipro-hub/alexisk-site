02 — About

Behind the light

I'm Alex Konstantinidis, also known as Alex Kayne. I direct commercials, music videos, fashion films and documentary, and I treat a thirty second spot with the same care as a feature: casting, light, rhythm, the cut.

From national campaigns to an intimate documentary about the people of Evros, the goal stays the same. One strong image, held for exactly as long as it needs.
