About

I'm Alex Konstantinidis, also known as Alex Kayne. I direct commercials, music videos, fashion films and documentary.

I treat a thirty second spot with the same care as a feature: casting, light, rhythm, the cut. From national campaigns for Lidl, Aperol and Stoiximan to music videos for Michalis Hatzigiannis and Paparizou, and a documentary about the people of Evros.
